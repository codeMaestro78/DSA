## Accuracy concerns

- Meta's official preparation page was rate-limited; do not treat a third-party mirror as current official evidence.
- Meesho's inspected internship campaign is closed and explicitly concerns 2024.
- NVIDIA's main onsite policy and FAQ have a scope ambiguity recorded in the brief.
- Global hiring guidance does not establish India-specific loops or campus eligibility.
- AI assistance policies differ by employer and assessment; preparation permission is not assessment permission.
- Job descriptions establish responsibilities for a requisition, not interview stages or universal early-career eligibility.
- Most hiring pages are undated. Current access does not establish a 2026 publication date.
- No company-question frequency, fastest vector database, private architecture or offer-probability claims are made.
