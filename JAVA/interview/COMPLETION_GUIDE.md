## Understand the practice inventories

- **44 original DSA exercises** in 22 guides: the initial32 plus12 extension exercises. The extension answers are reviewed traces, not additional submitted implementations.
- **22 public platform problems** in [the public catalog](03-DSA-and-Coding/PUBLIC_PROBLEM_CATALOG.md), separately scoped platform difficulty and no employer attribution.
- **134 original question-bank records** in [the metadata index](12-Interview-Question-Bank/Question_Bank_Index.md), with four editorial difficulties, hidden answers and related IDs.
- **Nine complete role/design sessions** in [the mock directory](13-Mock-Interviews/README.md), plus focused variants in role/domain chapters. Full-session candidate files are separate from interviewer answers; local variants put the review behind a disclosure.

Attempt before opening answers. Record contract, first approach, counterexample, tests actually run, hints and unresolved limits. Use [the scorecard](13-Mock-Interviews/Mock_Scorecard.md) and [rubric](13-Mock-Interviews/Interview_Evaluation_Rubric.md); an unread or unobserved dimension stays Not assessed. Reading a corrected answer does not establish independent mastery.

## What completion proves

[The Phase7 report](PHASE_7_REPORT.md) describes coverage delivered. [Whole-library QA](PHASE_7_QA_REPORT.md) records corrections, local executable checks, static HTML conversion and unexecuted boundaries. It does not establish every external URL current, every diagram browser-rendered, all native SQL rerun, a production integration or zero possible mistakes. Blank candidate/template fields are intentionally unfilled; no personal accomplishments or performance scores have been fabricated.

[Validation instructions](validation/README.md) give a one-command local suite runner. Historical phase reports preserve earlier counts and results rather than pretending later work existed then. The [final manifest](STRUCTURE_MANIFEST.md) is the current path-level inventory.
