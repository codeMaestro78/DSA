# Phase 5 practical question-bank delivery

Phase 5 delivers **134 original company-neutral questions across 13 banks**, a **22-problem public catalog**, a searchable metadata index and a study sequence. The full knowledge base remains incomplete: complete role mock packages are Phase 6; final whole-library QA is Phase 7. Unwritten specialist chapters remain visible in the manifest.

All records include category, difficulty, target roles, suggested round, experience, company scope, evaluated skills, sources and related IDs. Each prompt precedes a closed answer disclosure with expected answer, explanation, followups and common mistakes. Difficulty distribution: **25 Beginner, 60 Intermediate, 39 Advanced, 10 Expert**. Expert practice is optional specialist depth, not a universal early-career gate.

The [public catalog](03-DSA-and-Coding/PUBLIC_PROBLEM_CATALOG.md) covers all 22 DSA areas with public statement links, checked platform difficulties, original short summaries, mechanisms, complexity and edge cases. It does not copy full statements or supply submitted/accepted solutions. The earlier 32 original DSA exercises are linked separately and not counted again. A catalog entry does not complete a missing specialist teaching chapter.

## Navigation and company evidence

Start with [the study guide](PHASE_5_STUDY_GUIDE.md), then use [the question index](12-Interview-Question-Bank/Question_Bank_Index.md). Its [JSON metadata](12-Interview-Question-Bank/question_index.json) permits editor/script filtering without duplicating answers. The delivered interface is Markdown and metadata, not a filtering web application.

The [company preparation map](12-Interview-Question-Bank/Company_Preparation_Map.md) connects the Phase 4 dossiers to conditional role routes and five worked selections. **No original QB question is attributed to an employer.** Public platform statements, company responsibilities and candidate reports do not justify invented exact questions, universal loops or question-frequency estimates. A verified employer-question dataset remains an evidence gap rather than a fabricated deliverable.

Four track registers contribute **99 external source/context records**, **31 reused aliases** and **68 additions** to the central **272 accepted URL/scopes**. Local teaching references are excluded from those counts. Dates, reliability and scope remain per source; the count does not measure independent applicants or employer interview events. Sources were inspected on 2026-11-02; final integration continued into 2026-11-03 IST.

## Verification and remaining work

[The QA report](PHASE_5_QA_REPORT.md) records execution of isolated published Java/Python/NumPy examples, reviewed unexecuted SQL, synthetic arithmetic, cross-review corrections and structural checks. Reasoned design/behavioral answers are not deployed implementations. No provider calls, cloud deployment, browser-stream integration, model training, load benchmark or hidden-platform submission is claimed. Existing repository Java files were left untouched.

The library now has **375 Markdown files**, including **27 new Phase 5 documents**. Of the original 410 requested paths, **281 are delivered and 129 remain planned**; supporting documents do not change that denominator. All 14 originally requested Phase 5 bank/index paths are delivered. Directory existence and local checks do not establish an error-free or exhaustive curriculum.
