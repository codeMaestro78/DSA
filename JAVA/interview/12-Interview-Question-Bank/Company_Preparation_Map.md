# Company evidence to practical questions

Every QB question in this library is original, company-neutral **[INFERRED]** practice. **No QB question is attributed as an actual question asked by an employer.** The Phase 4 evidence does not establish a reliable, permission-cleared bank of exact employer prompts. A public problem's presence on a platform is also not evidence an employer used it. Company-question frequency, probabilities and internal pass marks remain unknown.

## Choose the actual opportunity first

Use the [complete company/regional directory](../01-Company-Research/Company_Comparison.md), covering all 37 named employer/regional dossiers plus the ecosystem analysis, and the [startup catalog](../01-Company-Research/Startups/Startup_Interview_Research_Catalog.md). Read the dossier's role, date, location, evidence type and unknowns before choosing exercises. A general company name cannot determine one preparation route.

For each application record the current requisition and invitation, confirmed assessment topics, allowed tools and unanswered recruiter questions. Then select the applicable role route below. A responsibility supports a study choice; it does not establish a round. Candidate self-reports remain scoped leads rather than official instructions. This map adds no new company facts; the linked dossiers hold their inspected primary sources and report limitations.

## What would justify a future attribution

Require a public attributable source, lawful/nonconfidential content, exact wording or a clearly marked paraphrase, publication and interview dates when known, role/level/location, and the source's status. Official samples establish published sample material only. A candidate report establishes what the author reported, not an independently verified event or a universal loop. Preserve conflicts and missing fields. Do not attach employer labels using a platform tag alone, leaked/private banks, a company product or an unsourced recollection.
