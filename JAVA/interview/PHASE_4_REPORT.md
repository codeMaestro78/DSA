## Sources and candidate accounts

The four Phase 4 registers contain **93 directly inspected records**: **78 official/company-context/employer-provided records** and **15 public candidate-report source pages**. Consolidation adds **67 accepted source URL/scopes**, reuses **26 indexed aliases**, and brings the central registry to **204 entries**.

A source-page count is not a count of independent candidates or interview events. A personal article reused across several dossiers is still one source. Some report pages contain several entries. Candidate identities, interview events and interpretations were not independently corroborated; selection is bounded and self-selected, not representative. Publication dates, interview dates, role, level and region are kept distinct when available.

No confidential questions were reproduced. No company-question frequency, difficulty distribution, pass threshold, offer probability or universal loop was inferred. The [source index](SOURCE_INDEX.md) and group registers record publication/update dates or gaps, access date, provenance, reliability and scope. Search-only, inaccessible and rejected leads remain outside accepted-source tables.

## Important findings and uncertainty

- Official public guides differ in detail and scope. Netflix's new-grad guide is limited to its published US program; Databricks' dated backend guide is not silently transferred to every early-career applicant. The dossiers preserve these boundaries.
- Meta's current SWE guide remained rate-limited. Readable candidate accounts are not labeled as an official replacement. Historical Stripe practice, a redirected Salesforce university route and unreadable Palantir process pages were not promoted to refreshed current policy.
- Perplexity and PostHog publish stage-specific AI permissions. Those distinctions are preserved rather than collapsed into a company-wide permission rule. Ask for the governing invitation before an assessment.
- Current x.ai careers text displays changed branding. The requested filename is retained with the observed identity caveat; no legal-transaction history is inferred from page text.
- The Google India example is a PhD-specific posting despite its early-career title. Meesho's current role-family overview is separated from a closed 2024 campaign. Neither a program list nor a title guarantees a suitable live opening.
- The startup catalog retains a posting's inconsistent location/visa fields and public-page timing differences as clarification needs. It does not resolve them by guessing.

These observations are supported within the linked dossiers and their source registers; they are not new broad rules about engineering hiring.

Phase 4 adds research documents and a local citation-bookkeeping checker.

## Accuracy limits and remaining work

First-party pages describe stated policies, not every actual interview. Undated pages are current-access snapshots, not necessarily recent publications. Job/report pages can change or disappear. Access today is not a guarantee of live availability tomorrow. Some employers provide only careers/team/posting context, leaving technical rounds and tool rules unverified.

Candidate reports remain uncorroborated observations. The study suggestions and exercises are original inferences, not employer questions or unpublished rubrics. No exhaustive external-link HTTP sweep, candidate identity audit or whole-employer process audit was performed. Existing Mermaid rendering and framework-execution gaps from earlier phases remain unchanged.

Start with [the Phase 4 study guide](PHASE_4_STUDY_GUIDE.md), choose an exact requisition, and use [the refresh protocol](01-Company-Research/RESEARCH_REFRESH_PROTOCOL.md) before applying or tailoring a mock.

**Next recommended phase: Phase 5 — practical question banks.** Broader role/domain/company indexing and reviewed answer sets remain to be built. Complete role mocks and final whole-library QA remain phases 6–7. The full knowledge base is still incomplete, and candidate readiness remains unassessed.
