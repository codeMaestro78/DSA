## Interview process

Record only supported stages and durations. Separate coding, design, domain-specific, behavioral and project discussions. Write “not established” where evidence is absent. Attach recruiter confirmation to the exact requisition, with its date, without publishing private correspondence.

## Responsibilities versus assessment

List responsibilities from the posting. Then separately list [INFERRED] preparation implications. A production ML responsibility does not prove an ML design round exists.

## Candidate reports

For each public report record role/level/location, interview/report dates, source, stages described and limitations. Reposts of the same account are not independent corroboration. Do not reproduce confidential questions.
