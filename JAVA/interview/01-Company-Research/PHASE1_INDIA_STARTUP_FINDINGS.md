# India and startup hiring: Phase 1 evidence brief

## Scope and proof boundary

Research cutoff and last verified: **2026-11-02**. This brief establishes usable official evidence and a research backlog. It is not the completed company catalog. A careers page confirms what its publisher says; it does not demonstrate how every interviewer behaves. Job listings change, and historical hiring events must retain their original scope.

No candidate-reported claims are used here. Public anecdotes found during discovery were not independently verified or combined into company-wide trends. No company-specific DSA question or numerical question frequency is established.

## Flipkart

**[OFFICIAL]** The public hiring guide describes application assessment, a possible technical phone screen depending on the role, onsite interviews, hiring committee review, and offer discussion. The guide explicitly assigns the recruiter the task of explaining the number of onsite rounds. It therefore supports a broad lifecycle, not a universal round count or duration. [How We Hire](https://www.flipkartcareers.com/howwehire), undated, accessed 2026-11-02.

**[OFFICIAL]** The engineering page presents separate preparation resources for Software Engineer, Senior Software Engineer, Engineering Manager, and Architect. These role distinctions are useful evidence that preparation should be level-specific. [Engineering at Flipkart](https://www.flipkartcareers.com/engineering), undated, accessed 2026-11-02.

**Access limitation:** A search result surfaced an older SDE PDF at `https://www.flipkartcareers.com/flipkart/assets/flipkart_pdf/SDE.pdf`, but opening it redirected to the careers homepage. Its cached text is not accepted here as a currently verified guide.

**[INFERRED] Preparation:** Practice explaining a coding invariant, testing boundary cases, and defending one project tradeoff. Ask the recruiter which design format applies to your level, which programming languages are permitted, and whether the process is for campus or experienced hiring. These are author-designed preparation tasks, not confirmed Flipkart questions.

## PhonePe

**[OFFICIAL]** The careers page names autonomy, learning, and impact as values and links to current openings. The openings page distinguishes engineering, analytics, and infrastructure roles; listings can have different experience requirements. These pages establish role discovery and company context, but the inspected material does not establish a standardized technical interview loop. [Careers](https://www.phonepe.com/careers/), [Job Openings](https://www.phonepe.com/careers/job-openings/), both undated, accessed 2026-11-02.

**[UNVERIFIED]** Fixed round counts, a mandatory machine-coding round for all engineers, and a single DSA difficulty distribution are not established by this evidence. No currently available 0–2 year software vacancy is promised by this brief.

**[INFERRED] Preparation:** Use a payment-status service as a practice domain: distinguish a retry from a duplicate transaction, explain an idempotency key, and show how you would reconcile conflicting provider responses. For infrastructure roles, add a latency investigation. These exercises develop relevant engineering judgment; they are not PhonePe interview reports. Ask for the exact job description and interview outline before changing the study plan.

## Razorpay

**[OFFICIAL]** The careers page describes three assessment themes: role fit, collaboration and working style, and ability to incorporate AI into workflows and problem solving. It also describes engineering work around AI-enabled payments. The page does not specify a universal count, order, duration, coding platform, or permitted AI tools. [Razorpay Careers](https://razorpay.com/careers/), undated, accessed 2026-11-02.

**[INFERRED] Preparation:** Be ready to demonstrate where AI accelerated a real task and how you checked its output. Pair that story with an independently explainable backend implementation: validate input, handle retries, enforce authorization, and describe a rollback. Ask whether the assessment allows AI assistance and what verification the candidate must provide. A public emphasis on AI does not itself grant permission to use an assistant during an interview.

**Accuracy concern:** Careers branding is evidence of stated assessment themes, not evidence that every role receives a separate round for each theme. Do not translate these headings into an invented three-round loop.

## Meesho: a useful historical counterexample

**[OFFICIAL; HISTORICAL]** A still-accessible SDE internship page states that applications are closed and identifies its internship period as February–July 2024. It describes screening, an online coding test, technical interviewing, and a hiring-manager assessment for that specific event. Its listed topics include OOP, data structures, databases, networking, debugging, design, and projects. [SDE Intern Open Recruitment](https://www.meesho.io/jobs/open-recruitment), event explicitly for 2024; publication date unstated; accessed 2026-11-02.

This is credible evidence for a past campaign. It is **not** evidence that applications are open in 2026 or that every current Meesho role uses those stages. Search engines can crawl an old page recently; a recent crawl is not a recent hiring event.

**[INFERRED] Preparation:** Use the historical topic breadth to build a balanced diagnostic, while asking for the current role's actual process. Research current early-career materials and a specific vacancy before writing the full company profile.

## Zoho

**[OFFICIAL]** Zoho's main careers page states that it values diverse interests, skills, and willingness to learn, and provides a route to open roles. The inspected page does not define one software engineering interview sequence. [Zoho Careers](https://www.zoho.com/careers/), undated, accessed 2026-11-02.

**[UNVERIFIED]** A universal aptitude test, fixed number of programming rounds, language restriction, and degree exemption for every opening are not established here. Hiring philosophy should not be rewritten as a guarantee of eligibility.

**[INFERRED] Preparation:** Explain a program without relying on framework vocabulary, test it with malformed input, and discuss a genuine example of learning a new area. Check the exact posting for eligibility and process details.

## Indian offices of global employers

**[UNVERIFIED]** This research pass does not establish India-specific interview variants for Google, Microsoft, or Amazon. Their global official guides may inform preparation, but do not prove a particular office's campus assessment, number of rounds, eligibility rules, or team-matching process. In the eventual India files, link to global evidence with its scope intact and record the missing regional evidence separately.

## Startups and YC: avoid category errors

**[OFFICIAL]** YC publishes an interview guide for founders applying to its accelerator. Its short partner interview is **not** a software engineer hiring process at YC portfolio companies. [YC Interview Guide](https://www.ycombinator.com/interviews), undated, accessed 2026-11-02.

**Research limitation:** Work at a Startup's homepage yielded no readable text, and an attempted handbook URL was inaccessible. Neither is treated as a verified employee interview guide. No universal seed, Series A, or AI-startup loop is asserted.

**[INFERRED] Research protocol:** For each startup, capture its specific role, location, seniority, current official job URL, assessment description, tool policy, and take-home scope. Funding stage is context, not proof of a format. Prepare to build and explain a small working feature, discuss customer impact, and clarify ambiguous requirements. Confirm whether any work trial is paid and how submission ownership is handled before undertaking it.
