# Refresh company research without losing scope

## When to refresh

Before applying or tailoring a mock, reopen the exact role/assessment sources that affect your decision. Record a new access date and publication/update date if visible. A same-day source inspection is still only a snapshot; a cached result or inaccessible body is not a refreshed policy.

This is an original [INFERRED] editorial protocol. It builds on [the methodology](../RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY.md), not an employer's hiring procedure.

## Source-change record

| Field | What to preserve |
|---|---|
| Claim and scope | Exact employer, role, level, location, stage and policy |
| Prior evidence | URL, publication/version and access date |
| Current access | Readable body, redirect, login wall, rate limit or failure |
| Change | New text, removed detail, identity/URL shift or unresolved inconsistency |
| Interpretation | What the new evidence supports; what it does not establish |
| Action | Amend the dossier, retain a historical note and ask a focused recruiter question |

## Handle common changes

A redirect to a general careers page cannot verify an old role-specific guide. A detailed source that disappears remains historical evidence, not a current requirement. If a company page changes branding, record the visible identity and application route; do not infer legal transactions or team continuity from the branding alone.

If two official pages differ in timing or labels, preserve both scopes and seek the applicable invitation. An invitation's task-specific tool policy must not be replaced by a general statement about AI-friendly culture. A model/API company's product is not permission to use an assistant during its assessment.

For job postings, distinguish basic from preferred qualifications, a program from a live vacancy and location from work authorization. If fields conflict, retain them rather than resolving them by guesswork.

## Candidate-account review

Read the actual public account. Record source/publication date, interview date if stated, role, level, region and whether it is firsthand. Do not convert relative words such as “November” into a year without evidence. A recent post can describe an old interview, and an application rejection does not establish a completed technical loop.

Avoid copying confidential questions, anonymous personal accusations or unnecessary identifying data. Retain the broad skill/format observation and its limitations. Deduplicate reposts; explain sample selection before describing recurrence. [Experience record](../14-Interview-Preparation-Templates/Interview_Experience_Template.md).

## Citation and publication check

Each company-specific fact needs a nearby source URL and evidence label. Source registers need title/publisher, URL, publication/update or explicit gap, access date, type/reliability and scope. Failed leads belong outside accepted-source tables. Original exercises must remain company-neutral unless supported attribution is deliberately established.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] A directly readable source supports the exact claim, not just the page title.
- [ ] Role, location, date and stage are preserved.
- [ ] Tool permissions and eligibility remain current scoped facts or unknown.
- [ ] Historical statements are not silently promoted to live policy.
- [ ] Candidate accounts are distinct from official claims and frequency data.
- [ ] Local links/manifest/status are updated without claiming external truth from a structural checker.
