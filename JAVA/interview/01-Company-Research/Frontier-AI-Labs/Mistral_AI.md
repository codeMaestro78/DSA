## Official process: separate engineering from GTM

[OFFICIAL] For **science, product and engineering**, the page describes an initial recruiter/hiring-manager conversation, **typically two to five technical exercises**, and a values conversation; it says references are checked before an offer. GTM/corporate has its own outline, including functional discussions and possible role-based exercises. These are published role-family descriptions, not a fixed count for every applicant. [Interviewing with us](https://mistral.ai/careers/).

No universal coding language, problem difficulty, take-home duration or AI-tool policy was established. Technical exercises “reflect real challenges” does not establish a private task or authorize tool use. Obtain written assessment-specific instructions from the recruiting contact.

## Preparation plan and drill

[INFERRED] Must prepare coding clarity, tests and project ownership. Important for science/ML: a small reproducible experiment and honest account of what changed. Important for product/solutions: an acceptance criterion and deployment failure path. Advanced optimization/kernel/distributed content should follow a confirmed role rather than crowd out fundamentals for a 0–2 year candidate.

**Original drill — MIS-P4-01, Intermediate; company-neutral applied-AI design:** a customer wants a faster assistant, but your first experiment improves latency while reducing supported answers. Explain the decision.

<details><summary>Expected reasoning and followups</summary>

Define task quality, first useful output/completion latency, workload and token/cost budget. Compare retrieval, context and model changes independently using labeled cases, including unanswerable questions. Consider a smaller safe scope or an evaluated fallback rather than hiding the regression in an average. Followups: rollout gate, rollback and what evidence would reverse your choice. Weak answer: treating any shorter response as a universally better system.
