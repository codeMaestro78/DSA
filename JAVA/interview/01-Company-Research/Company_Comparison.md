# Compare companies using scoped evidence

## What this comparison means

This is an evidence-navigation and preparation tool, not an employer ranking, a difficulty league table or a probability-of-offer model. The dossiers distinguish official guidance, candidate self-reports, original preparation inferences and unresolved facts. A company with more readable public documentation is not automatically an easier, better or more suitable employer.

The folders are editorial navigation categories. An India-specific dossier is a regional evidence record, not proof that the global process applies unchanged. The ecosystem page is analysis rather than a separate employer. The startup catalog adds six named cases without assigning unverified funding rounds.

## Compare the exact opportunity

For each target record company/employer identity, requisition, role/level, team/location, qualifications, source date, assessment format, allowed tools and remaining questions. Use [the company template](../14-Interview-Preparation-Templates/Company_Research_Template.md). Responsibilities and qualifications do not automatically establish interview stages.

| Evidence you have | What it supports | Preparation decision [INFERRED] |
|---|---|---|
| Current role-specific official assessment guide | Stated skills, format and restrictions within that guide's scope | Rehearse that format and verify the invitation matches |
| Broad official hiring guide | Published lifecycle and variation | Ask which parts apply to your requisition |
| Official job description | Posted responsibilities and eligibility | Match relevant skills and check suitability; leave rounds unknown |
| Careers/values page only | Company-described context | Prepare honest motivation/project evidence; request format details |
| Dated candidate account | One person's reported experience | Generate recruiter questions and optional practice variations |
| Historical or inaccessible source | A limited lead or older statement | Retain date/access limit; obtain fresh evidence before relying on it |

No row authorizes treating a company's tech stack as a language restriction, a product capability as a hiring requirement, or one account's outcome as a pass threshold.

## Role-based differences

The following are original [INFERRED] study implications. They are not verified employer loops.

- SDE/backend: prioritize independent coding, correctness/tests, SQL/API and concurrency reasoning.
- Applied AI/full-stack: retain that core and add evaluated model output, retrieval/tool permissions, request/stream lifecycle and failure recovery.
- ML/research: use the posting to choose mathematical depth, valid experiments, reproducibility and training/serving work. Do not infer a universal degree or paper requirement from a title.
- Infrastructure: emphasize request/runtime paths, bounded concurrency, incident diagnosis and evidence-based capacity/recovery.
- FDE/customer work: add discovery, integration, acceptance criteria and clear stakeholder explanation. Confirm actual title, seniority and location.

Use [the role comparison](../02-Role-Based-Preparation/Role_Comparison.md) and its canonical chapters; avoid studying every specialist track for every employer.

## Early-career suitability

A 0–2-year candidate should separately check degree, graduation window, experience type, work location/authorization and supported onboarding. “Early career,” “all levels,” “remote” and “research” each need the full posting's context. If a requirement is missing or inconsistent, preserve the uncertainty and ask; do not silently waive it or assume all other roles share it.

## Candidate reports and uncertainty

The source registers retain candidate role/level/location and report/interview dates when available. Missing context stays missing; several reports or comments from one account are not independent corroboration. These are bounded, self-selected observations, not a representative survey. No company-frequency percentages, difficulty distributions or private question lists are established.

When sources disagree, compare role, location, level and dates first. If the conflict remains, record it and ask for the governing instructions. Do not average round counts or choose a preferred account just because it matches the desired preparation plan.

## Practice — COMP-P4-01

**Intermediate; all target roles; company-neutral research/preparation exercise.** Pick one employer with an official guide and one with careers-only evidence. Write three supported facts, three unanswered questions and a one-week preparation plan for each.

<details><summary>Expected reasoning and followup</summary>

The plan should preserve shared fundamentals, add only justified role-specific depth and identify what the recruiter must resolve. Evidence gaps change confidence about format, not the truth of a guessed loop. Followup: which new answer would materially change your study allocation? Common mistake: ranking company difficulty or predicting a question from sparse anecdotes.

</details>
