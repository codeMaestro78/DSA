# Indian technology hiring: an evidence-based preparation map

## Scope and why categories are insufficient

**Last verified: 2026-11-02.** This is original [INFERRED] analysis of the scoped evidence in the India dossiers. It is not a census of Indian jobs, funding stages, salaries or interview frequencies. “Indian startup,” “product company” and “global capability centre” are context labels; none determines a universal engineering interview loop.

A candidate with 0–2 years of experience should begin with the exact requisition and demonstrated skills, not a presumed company ranking. Separate employer identity, product/team, location, seniority, hiring channel, qualifications and assessment instructions. Two teams in one company can need different work, while similarly named companies may be unrelated.

## What the inspected evidence actually shows

| Example | Supported observation | Inference to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| [Flipkart](https://www.flipkartcareers.com/howwehire) | Official broad lifecycle; recruiter explains onsite round count | Every campus/off-campus engineer has the same number of rounds |
| [Groww](https://groww.in/careers) | Official application, screening, interviews and offer categories | “Interviews” means a fixed number of machine-coding/design sessions |
| [Meesho](https://www.meesho.io/early-careers) | Official early-career role families | Every listed family is a live vacancy with identical eligibility |
| [Google India PhD role](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/107018346561446598-software-engineer-phd-early-career-2026) | A specific early-career-titled role requires a PhD | Early-career always means any recent undergraduate qualifies |
| [CRED security role](https://jobs.lever.co/cred/9a746e68-0858-4190-9144-54b19a2cbf2c) | A Bengaluru security requisition specifies its own experience/domain | A security posting supplies the generic backend or graduate loop |
| [Eternal](https://www.eternal.com/) | Multiple distinct businesses under the group | Zomato, Blinkit and the parent necessarily share a hiring process |

These are narrow publisher statements. No aggregate market size, vacancy count, compensation distribution or probability of success follows from them.

## Preparation matrix

The recommendations below are [INFERRED], company-neutral and should be adjusted to recruiter-confirmed scope.

| Work family | Shared foundation | Useful extension | Evidence to produce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early-career SDE/backend | DSA, language fluency, SQL/API correctness and testing | Component design, concurrency and failure recovery | Correct implementation, invariant, counterexample and tests |
| Full-stack/product | Backend contracts plus frontend state/identity | Streaming/reconnect and user-visible failure states | A complete authorized request lifecycle |
| Applied AI/RAG | Backend correctness, retrieval and evaluation | Citation/permission/deletion and bounded tools | Labeled quality evidence and a failure trace |
| ML/data science | Mathematics, valid splits, baselines and metrics | Experiment design and reproducible training/serving | A leakage-free experiment with uncertainty and limits |
| Infrastructure/security | Linux/networking, reliability and authority | Capacity/incident investigation and narrow automation | Observation-to-hypothesis reasoning and a safe verified fix |
| Customer/deployment work | Engineering plus communication | Discovery, integration and acceptance/handoff | A real or explicitly simulated bounded delivery scenario |

This matrix does not assert every company uses these exact titles or assesses every skill in a dedicated round. Advanced topics are optional until responsibilities and preparation diagnostics justify them.

## Campus, off-campus and experience boundaries

Treat a campaign as a dated event. The [closed Meesho 2024 internship page](https://www.meesho.io/jobs/open-recruitment) cannot establish current availability or compensation. The current early-career overview and that historical event are different sources with different scope.

For each target, record graduation/degree requirements, accepted experience types, location/work authorization, active application route and stage instructions. Do not assume internships count as non-internship experience, that a broad hiring philosophy overrides a posting, or that a role title implies eligibility. Missing information is a recruiter question, not permission to fill it from memory or a job aggregator.

## Candidate accounts without false trends

This research includes a bounded set of public self-reports for Flipkart, PhonePe, Razorpay and Swiggy. Their dates, role/region limits and provenance are in the individual dossiers and [source register](PHASE4_SOURCES.md). They do not form a representative sample. Cross-posts from the same author are not independent corroboration, and an assessment-invitation thread is not a completed-loop account.

Use accounts to generate questions such as “Does this apply to my role?” rather than to promise a particular prompt or round. Tool permissions must come from current assessment instructions; an employer's AI branding or one candidate's AI-assisted exercise does not grant universal permission.

## Original research exercise — IN4-ECOSYSTEM

**Difficulty:** Intermediate. **Target:** early-career SDE/AI/full-stack/ML/FDE planning. **Category/round:** role selection and preparation; company-neutral [INFERRED].

Choose two real postings with different work families. Build a table of observed requirements, your actual evidence, unassessed skills and questions that materially change preparation. Do not rank the employers by rumor.

<details><summary>Expected reasoning and followups</summary>

A sound comparison separates responsibilities from assessment stages, identifies one weak important prerequisite and proposes a bounded practice task. Attach a source/date to each employer fact and an artifact to each claimed skill. Mark unknowns rather than manufacturing a matching profile.

Followup: what changes if the “early-career” posting requires a different degree? Reconsider eligibility or select another route without changing the source facts. Common mistake: treating a company label, title or old campus campaign as a current universal rule.

</details>
