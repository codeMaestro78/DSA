# India company dossiers

## Purpose and evidence boundary

Phase 4 delivers fourteen named-employer/region dossiers plus an India hiring-preparation map. Each separates official scoped facts, public candidate self-reports, original preparation and unresolved questions. Evidence strength varies; an evidence-limited dossier does not invent a missing interview loop.

Research/access cutoff: **2026-11-02**. Confirm the exact requisition and tool policy before applying. A global hiring guide is not proof of India-specific stages. A closed campaign, role-family overview or product page is not a live eligible vacancy.

## Read in this order

1. Use [the ecosystem map](Indian_Startup_Ecosystem.md) to select a specific role and research unit.
2. Read the relevant employer dossier and its unknowns.
3. Attempt the company-neutral drill before opening its answer.
4. Record recruiter-confirmed scope privately using [the research template](../../14-Interview-Preparation-Templates/Company_Research_Template.md).

## Key cautions

- Meesho’s 2024 internship campaign is historical; its current early-career overview has a different scope.
- Zomato is distinguished from other Eternal businesses; group context does not define a shared loop.
- The inspected Google early-career posting requires a PhD; title wording alone does not establish eligibility.
- Zepto careers access failed; its app listing supports product identity, not an engineering interview process.
- Candidate reports are bounded observations, not question-frequency statistics or universal policy.
