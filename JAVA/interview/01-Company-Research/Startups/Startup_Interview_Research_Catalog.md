# Startup research catalog

## How to use this sample

This is a small evidence catalog, not a ranking, exhaustive startup list or jobs recommendation. No current funding-round assignment is made for these employers. Each accepted source was directly read on 2026-11-02; most are undated. Role availability, location and eligibility require a fresh check before application.

## Linear — detailed public hiring guidance

[OFFICIAL] Doug Parker's **April 28, 2026** article describes an application/conversation/interview process with a final paid work trial, normally 2–5 days. The article says sequencing varies by role and describes coding/architecture/design topics as engineering examples. It reports typical rather than guaranteed process timings. [How we hire](https://linear.app/now/how-we-hire-at-linear).

[UNVERIFIED] The article does not establish that every engineering posting accepts 0–2 years or permits AI in each stage. Ask for the exact assessment guide, language/tools and review expectations.

[INFERRED] Prepare a bounded feature plus a clear account of its user value, design decisions, tests and remaining work. A long trial is a commitment to clarify in advance; do not assume another startup has the same policy.

## PostHog — candidate page and engineering-specific handbook

[OFFICIAL] Its careers page publishes a staged process with a paid SuperDay. The general and engineering handbooks add role-specific detail. Their labels and technical-screen durations differ: the candidate page says 45 minutes, while the engineering handbook describes an hour. Preserve both and ask which guide applies to the requisition. [Careers](https://posthog.com/careers), [general handbook](https://posthog.com/handbook/people/hiring-process), [engineering handbook](https://posthog.com/handbook/people/hiring-process/engineering-hiring).

[OFFICIAL] The engineering page allows AI for the SuperDay project but not its debugging session. Its general experience language still defers role requirements to the job specification. [Engineering handbook](https://posthog.com/handbook/people/hiring-process/engineering-hiring).

[INFERRED] Practice prioritizing a small full-stack deliverable and explaining code without relying on a generator. Do not collapse stage-specific permissions into one company-wide tool rule.

## Cursor — product/role discovery, process unestablished

[OFFICIAL] The inspected careers page describes its developer-software context and links roles. [Careers](https://cursor.com/careers), undated. No usable interview sequence or stage-specific AI-assistance rule was established from that page.

[INFERRED] Select the exact role first, then prepare relevant coding, project and design evidence. Ask whether the assessment is practical implementation, algorithms, debugging or another format. Product identity alone does not grant assessment tool permission.

## LangChain — operating principles, process unestablished

[OFFICIAL] Its careers page publishes operating principles and describes an agent-engineering product context. [Careers](https://www.langchain.com/careers), undated. That material does not establish a universal LangGraph coding round or a current early-career opening.

[INFERRED] Prepare an evaluated workflow with bounded state/actions and explain the framework-independent contracts. A useful project discussion distinguishes a library abstraction from durability, authorization and real task quality.

## Temporal — responsibilities and logistics context

[OFFICIAL] Its careers page describes remote work with country/region and occasional office/travel distinctions, and lists technical roles. It notes on-call responsibilities for relevant teams. [Careers](https://temporal.io/careers), undated. This is context and role discovery, not an inspected engineering interview loop.

[INFERRED] For a relevant systems/backend role, prepare one retry/idempotency or workflow-recovery example and a truthful incident/debugging discussion. Verify experience, location, on-call scope and assessment format for that exact posting.

## Respan — scoped YC full-stack posting

[OFFICIAL] The inspected employer listing on YC describes full-stack work spanning APIs/data pipelines and a React/Python-oriented product. It asks 3+ years. India/remote-India location text appears alongside a US-citizen/visa-only field; the combination needs recruiter clarification. [Posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/respan/jobs/8s4G7k6-software-engineer-full-stack), undated.

[UNVERIFIED] No interview stages, language restrictions or AI-assistance permission were established. This is not asserted to be an eligible opening for a 0–2-year candidate.

[INFERRED] Prepare a small data-backed feature and one query/trace debugging explanation. Keep the role's posted constraints and uncertain logistics separate from transferable study topics.
