# Researching YC startup engineering roles

## Two different activities

[OFFICIAL] YC's startup-jobs page is a discovery route for jobs at YC companies. Its listings distinguish companies, job titles and locations. This does not establish a shared employee interview process or eligibility for every listing. [YC jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs), undated, accessed 2026-11-02.

An accelerator's founder-admissions interview and an employee's hiring assessment have different subjects and decision criteria. Do not use a founder interview duration as a software engineering loop. The previously indexed founder guide returned no readable body in this pass, so no refreshed process details are asserted from it.

## Use the exact posting

[OFFICIAL] The inspected Respan full-stack listing identifies YC batch W24 and asks for 3+ years. It also displays India/Remote India alongside a US-citizen/visa-only field. Those location/eligibility fields require clarification; neither a batch label nor a remote label resolves them. [Scoped posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/respan/jobs/8s4G7k6-software-engineer-full-stack), undated, accessed 2026-11-02. This is not a promised 0–2-year opportunity.

The [catalog](Startup_Interview_Research_Catalog.md) retains that example's technical responsibilities and unknown process rather than fabricating rounds.

## Research workflow

[INFERRED] Capture employer/title/location, experience requirements, current source URL, technical responsibilities, known assessment description and unanswered questions. Follow the employer-linked application route, then request the actual language/editor, documentation/AI policy, take-home budget and evaluated skills. Keep application records private and do not enter personal data into this public knowledge base.

Use funding stage and batch as context only when verified. A company's batch year does not determine current team size, funding round or engineering maturity. A job-board snapshot is also not a guarantee that a vacancy remains available later.

## Preparation exercise

Choose one real listing and separate its statements into responsibilities, eligibility, process and unknowns. Map only the important responsibilities to evidence you actually have: a correct implementation, evaluated AI workflow, integration or project tradeoff. Mark adjacent experience and unassessed skills honestly.

## Practice — STAGE-P4-04

**Beginner/Intermediate; SDE/AI/full-stack; company-neutral role-research discussion; 0–2 years.** A job is listed as remote but its location/visa fields conflict. What do you conclude?

<details><summary>Expected reasoning, followup and common mistake</summary>

Record the inconsistency and ask the recruiter which legal work location and authorization apply. Do not infer unrestricted international eligibility, silently discard a requirement or promise a visa. Followup: which preparation can proceed while logistics are unresolved? General coding/project practice can continue; application suitability remains unknown.

</details>
