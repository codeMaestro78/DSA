# Phase 4: use company dossiers effectively

## Goal

Turn employer evidence into a focused preparation plan for an exact opportunity. Phase 4 supplies researched dossiers, not a promise that a particular role is open, suitable or likely to make an offer. The learning sequence below is original [INFERRED] guidance for your stated early-career profile.

## First session

1. Choose one actual requisition and open its [company dossier](01-Company-Research/Company_Comparison.md).
2. Record employer/title, location, level, qualifications, source URL and verification date using [the research form](14-Interview-Preparation-Templates/Company_Research_Template.md).
3. Separate responsibilities, eligibility, confirmed assessment details and unknowns. A role description supplies no missing interview rounds.
4. Write three recruiter questions that could change your preparation: format/domain depth, language/execution environment and stage-specific resource/AI policy are useful starting points.
5. Attempt one company-neutral exercise from the dossier, then read its expected reasoning. Do not treat the exercise as a reported employer question.

## Read evidence in this order

| Evidence | How to use it |
|---|---|
| Current role-specific official guide/invitation | Confirm scope, skills and restrictions for the actual task |
| Broad official hiring guide | Establish the published lifecycle and ask about variation |
| Exact job posting | Check responsibilities/eligibility and select relevant depth |
| Careers/team/values page | Understand publisher-described context, leaving process gaps visible |
| Dated candidate report | Generate contextual questions and optional variations, preserving its limits |
| Search excerpt, inaccessible or older page | Treat as a lead/historical record until verified appropriately |

Read report date and interview date separately. Preserve title/location/level context, contradictions and access failures. No company-question encounter probability follows from a few reports or tags.

## Choose a manageable target set

Start with one main role family and a small shortlist, rather than trying to master all specialist topics for every employer. Keep DSA, backend correctness and honest project explanation as the shared core. Use exact responsibilities to add ML/research, infrastructure, full-stack/AI or customer-engineering depth.

For a 0–2-year profile, verify degree/graduation/experience and location/work-authorization requirements. A title such as early-career or a heading such as all-levels cannot override an explicit qualification. Do not treat a senior FDE or PhD-specific role as a generic graduate opening.

## One-week tailoring exercise

Use your available time, with the [master roadmap](MASTER_INTERVIEW_ROADMAP.md) as the longer plan. A suggested 12-hour week might allocate four hours to unfamiliar coding attempts, three to the confirmed role's technical/design focus, two to truthful project/behavioral followups, one to environment rehearsal, one to source/recruiter clarification and one to error-log revision. Reallocate when the actual loop gives stronger evidence. These are study budgets, not employer requirements.

Record completed attempts and hints in [the tracker](PROGRESS_TRACKER.md). A read dossier or accepted copied solution is not independent performance.

## Refresh and readiness

Use [the refresh protocol](01-Company-Research/RESEARCH_REFRESH_PROTOCOL.md) before an application or assessment. When instructions are incomplete, keep the question open rather than assuming tool permission. Continue independent foundational practice while waiting for clarification.

A useful readiness gate is that you can explain the role, demonstrate its most important skills, defend a project claim and identify exactly what remains unknown about the assessment. Hiring outcomes and hidden rubrics remain outside this self-assessment.

## Remaining work

Phase 5 is the broader practical question-bank phase. Complete role mocks and final whole-library review remain phases 6–7. Current dossier exercises and local document QA do not substitute for those deliverables.
