# Forward deployed engineering preparation roadmap

## Role focus and prerequisites

This chapter gives original **[INFERRED]**, company-neutral preparation for an early-career Forward Deployed Engineer. It does not establish a company's interview format or imply eligibility for a particular posting. Read the linked canonical lesson for the mechanism, then use this chapter to practice the role's decisions and evidence.

## Understand the decision

Prepare to turn a customer's uncertain need into a useful, integrated and operable result. The title does not specify one universal process. Distinguish software implementation, technical discovery, customer coordination and production operation; individual postings weight these differently.

[OFFICIAL] OpenAI's inspected SF FDSWE posting describes customer/full-stack delivery and asks 7+ years; Anthropic's Paris FDE posting asks 8+ years and French fluency. Databricks' US AI FDE posting says “ALL LEVELS” but explicitly excludes new-graduate/entry-level candidates. Those are scoped posting facts, not proof about all roles. For 0–2 years, use the transferable exercises here while verifying eligibility per requisition.

## Worked role scenario

An early-career candidate can demonstrate a small authorized integration, an honest customer-discovery simulation and a clear deployment/debugging plan. They should not present the simulation as an enterprise deployment or infer that the inspected senior postings fit their experience.

## Interview practice: FDE-P3-01

- **Category / difficulty:** Forward deployed engineering preparation roadmap / Intermediate.
- **Target role / experience:** Forward Deployed Engineer; approximately 0–2 years, with advanced followups optional.
- **Round / company:** Suggested technical, project or design practice; company-neutral.
- **Evaluates:** Whether you can apply the chapter's mechanism, identify a failure boundary and justify a decision using evidence.
- **Prompt:** How would you prepare for FDE without inventing customer experience?
- **Related:** The canonical lesson and adjacent chapters linked below.

<details><summary>Expected reasoning, followup and common mistake — open after attempting</summary>

**Expected answer:** Use real collaboration/integration episodes and explicitly hypothetical customer exercises. Practice discovery, a bounded prototype, API/data contracts and evidence-based diagnosis. Verify the specific role and eligibility.

**Followup:** How is this different from assuming every FDE interview has a customer round?

**Common mistake:** Treating a role responsibility or job title as evidence of an interview stage.

**Evidence boundary:** State what you personally implemented or checked. A plausible design or passing local fixture is not evidence of live production behavior.

</details>
