# Full mock interviews

These are editorial **[INFERRED]** practice scenarios for early-career preparation. They are not exact employer questions, standard company loops or hiring thresholds. Optional advanced branches do not replace the core task.

## Scoring and administration

- [Common rubric v1.0](Interview_Evaluation_Rubric.md): six dimensions with the same 0–4 meaning and task-specific anchors in every companion.
- [Blank scorecard](Mock_Scorecard.md): conditions, task evidence, hint ledger, findings and next practice.
- [Reviewer calibration](Calibration_Exercises.md): synthetic examples of supplied help, unresolved defects and unobserved outcomes.
- [Machine-readable mock inventory](mock_index.json): paired paths, role metadata and stage durations; no solutions.

Clarifications are H0; progressively stronger directional hints are H1/H2/H3. Record assistance and actual effects rather than applying an automatic global deduction. Leave unobserved dimensions Not assessed. A 4 requires repeated independent variations, and no practice total predicts a hiring decision. All fictional workloads and customer roles stay fictional.
