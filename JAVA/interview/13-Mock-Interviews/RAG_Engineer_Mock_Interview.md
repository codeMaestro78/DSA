## Scenario and contract

You own a multi-tenant policy assistant. A corpus change increased the number of plausible-looking answers, but users report obsolete advice and one cross-tenant excerpt. Diagnose the current retrieval/evaluation contract and propose a bounded migration. A retrieved text can be relevant to a question yet unauthorized or obsolete.

For this fixture, current access is known and there is one correct eligible document for each answerable query. “Top-2” means the two raw candidates actually returned; an unauthorized candidate appearing here has already been exposed to the answering component and is a policy failure, even if not cited. Do not treat that exposure as acceptable production behavior.

| Document | Tenant visibility | Version status | Content purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | A only | Obsolete | Old A refund policy |
| A2 | A only | Current | Current A refund policy |
| B1 | B only | Current | Current B refund policy |
| P | Public | Current | Public account-setup instructions |

| Query | User tenant | Correct eligible document | Raw top-2 | Draft cites |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| q1: current A refund policy | A | A2 | A1, B1 | A1 |
| q2: current B refund policy | B | B1 | B1, P | B1 |
| q3: unpublished A exception | A | None; must abstain | P, A2 | A2 |
| q4: account setup | A | P | P, A2 | P |

Define answerability coverage as the number of queries with at least one eligible gold document divided by all four queries; this measures dataset answerability, not the system's rate of answering.

All fixture judgments are stipulated, not generated by a model. We are not given real text needed to check entailment. Do not invent it. For answerable queries define Recall@2 as eligible gold documents retrieved divided by eligible gold documents; report its macro mean over answerable queries only. Report unsupported-query behavior separately.

## Candidate tasks

### RAG-T1 — Define valid evidence

Identify which corpus metadata and authorization/version rules must be applied, what “correct” means for this assistant and what evidence you need beyond a citation ID. Trace one query.

### RAG-T2 — Diagnose the fixture

Compute answerability coverage, Recall@2 and policy failures using the stated denominators. Explain which drafts are unacceptable and why. Decide whether a reranker-only change can fix q1 under the raw top-2 candidate set.

### RAG-T3 — Plan a safe corpus change

Propose a minimal shadow evaluation and atomic publication/rollback approach for a new embedding/index version. Include document identity, chunk/version provenance, tenant policy, deletes and caches. Define what remains unavailable if permission is revoked while an answer is generating.

### RAG-T4 — Communicate and defend

Give a two-minute incident update with confirmed fixture facts, hypotheses and next checks. The product owner requests “100% grounded answers” after the migration; explain a measurable acceptance contract and its limits. Answer one followup selected by the interviewer.
