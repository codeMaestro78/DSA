# Phase 6 full mock interview delivery

Phase 6 delivers **nine complete original mock sessions**, **nine separate interviewer companions**, and a common evidence-based scoring package. Together they contain **36 connected task stages** and **645 minutes of live practice**. Each session is 60 or 75 minutes, with preflight and a separate 10–15-minute debrief outside that budget. Durations are editorial choices, not employer timing claims.

Each candidate packet supplies the contract, fixtures, questions, requested artifacts and four-stage timing. Each companion provides fixed H0 answers, checkpoints, progressive hints, stuck/finished followup branches, worked reasoning or code, valid alternatives, mistakes, strong indicators, task-specific anchors and remediation. No solution disclosure is embedded in the candidate file. File separation is a practice boundary, not access control against opening a spoiler.

All workloads, customers, labels and incident outcomes are stipulated fictional inputs. The frontier and startup sessions do not reproduce an employer's loop. The tasks are distinct integrated scenarios rather than reprinted Phase 5 records; they do not increase the 134-question bank count.

## Scoring and use

Start with [the study guide](PHASE_6_STUDY_GUIDE.md) and [mock directory](13-Mock-Interviews/README.md). [Rubric v1.0](13-Mock-Interviews/Interview_Evaluation_Rubric.md) uses CONTRACT, REASONING, CORRECTNESS, VALIDATION, RISK and COMMUNICATION, with the same 0–4 meanings across roles. Unobserved dimensions stay **Not assessed**. Level 4 requires repeated independent transfer evidence; one polished session is insufficient. No total, hiring cutoff or offer probability is supplied.

The [blank scorecard](13-Mock-Interviews/Mock_Scorecard.md) records conditions, hints, task evidence and followup. [Three calibration exercises](13-Mock-Interviews/Calibration_Exercises.md) distinguish supplied mechanisms, unresolved correctness and hypothetical delivery. They are reviewed synthetic guidance, not a real candidate sample or validated scoring study. Pair, solo and explicitly assisted modes have different evidence limits; no candidate result or personal accomplishment is prefilled.
