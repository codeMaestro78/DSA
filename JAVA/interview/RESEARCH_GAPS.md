## Evidence gaps

- Many companies expose careers or engineering pages without a public, role-specific interview loop. Do not fill missing stages using general reputation.
- Phase 4 includes 15 public candidate-report source pages in a bounded, self-selected sample. This is not a systematic/representative dataset. Stage counts retain source scope; company DSA frequency, difficulty distributions and unsupported regional rules remain unestablished.
- Current vacancy and entry-level eligibility checks must happen per posting at application time. This phase researches guidance and role expectations, not a live jobs inventory.
- AI vendor SDK versions, model names, pricing and benchmark rankings are deliberately not frozen before exercises require them.
- [The Phase 3 vector-store comparison](02-Role-Based-Preparation/RAG-Engineer/Vector_Databases.md) now documents seven stores with primary references. No live comparison or “fastest” recommendation is established; selection still requires version-pinned workload evidence.
- Internal ChatGPT, frontier-training or hiring-committee details cannot be inferred from public product behavior.
- Phase 5 original prompts have no verified employer attribution. [Company routes](12-Interview-Question-Bank/Company_Preparation_Map.md) preserve this evidence gap rather than inventing exact questions or frequency.
