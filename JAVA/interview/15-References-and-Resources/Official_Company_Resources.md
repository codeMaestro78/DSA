# Official company resources and scope

Use an official employer page to learn what that page actually states: a public assessment theme, a candidate policy, a role responsibility or a location-specific qualification. A job description does not establish round counts; a product stack does not establish a required interview language.

## Curated routes and limits

| Resource | How to use it | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Microsoft technical guidance](https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/hiring-tips/technical-interviewing) | Clarification, coding and testing themes | Published guidance, not this library’s rubric or every role’s loop |
| [Amazon software topics](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/interview-prep/software-development-topics) | Pick applicable fundamentals | Ask which topics apply to the actual requisition |
| [Company comparison](../01-Company-Research/Company_Comparison.md) | Navigate every scoped company/regional dossier | Dossiers preserve dates, role/location and report reliability |
| [Refresh protocol](../01-Company-Research/RESEARCH_REFRESH_PROTOCOL.md) | Recheck a target before an application | A prior readable source is not a current vacancy guarantee |


## Use the material actively

Record the exact URL, publication/access date, role/level/location and what remains unknown. Distinguish official guidance from anonymous candidate accounts and original preparation inferences. Do not reproduce leaked/confidential prompts or invent question-frequency percentages. The existing dossiers, rather than a new universal company list, hold per-employer evidence.

## Practice — RESOURCE-P7-01

A posting says “experience with distributed systems.” Can you record a mandatory distributed-design interview round?

<details><summary>Expected reasoning — attempt first</summary>

No. It supports a responsibility/qualification in the posting’s scope. Ask for the current assessment format and prepare the relevant fundamentals provisionally; leave the round unknown until supported. A preparation choice and a confirmed employer stage are different records.

Followup: name one artifact you would retain and one unsupported claim you would refuse to make. Choose the smallest exercise that tests transfer instead of collecting another unrelated resource.

</details>
