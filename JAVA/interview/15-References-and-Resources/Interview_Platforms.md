## Use the material actively

Use the public statement’s constraints as the contract. Record independent attempt, hints, compiler/runtime and tests actually run. An accepted submission can miss the explanation/edge-case reasoning needed in a conversation; local tests can miss platform-specific hidden cases. In a real assessment follow the current invitation’s language, documentation and AI rules, not the looser settings of your practice session.
