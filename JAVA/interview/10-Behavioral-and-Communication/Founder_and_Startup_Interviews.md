# Founder and startup conversations

## Start from the actual role

There is no verified universal startup interview loop in this knowledge base. Funding stage alone does not prove that an employer uses a take-home, pair programming or a work trial. Ask the company about the specific process. YC's founder admissions interview is not employee hiring at its portfolio companies; see [the research brief](../01-Company-Research/PHASE1_INDIA_STARTUP_FINDINGS.md).

## Prepare product-oriented explanations

[INFERRED] Practice connecting a technical choice to a user outcome, deadline and maintenance cost. A founder conversation may be a useful setting for this practice, but the recommendation is not a claim that every founder assesses the same traits.

For a vague request such as “add AI search,” clarify who uses it, what current workflow fails, which data is authorized, how success will be measured and which deadline is real. Propose a bounded initial experiment instead of committing to a complex agent platform before understanding the need.

## Questions for mutual evaluation

What would the first useful delivery look like? Who owns product decisions and technical review? Which infrastructure and customer constraints already exist? How are incidents handled? What support exists for an early-career engineer? How is a take-home scoped, what data may be used and who can access the submission?

These questions help establish expectations. They do not imply a particular company lacks support or has an unsafe process.

## Hypothetical practice

You have one week to demonstrate document search with citations. Describe a small read-only scope, test dataset, success criteria, data permissions and what you would defer. Explain what a prototype can and cannot prove. Do not estimate a delivery time without stating assumptions about data, access and existing code.

<details><summary>Expected reasoning</summary>

A good answer reduces uncertainty, identifies the smallest useful outcome and defines a review point. It preserves correctness and permission boundaries while narrowing scope. “Use the latest agent framework” does not answer the product question.

</details>

## Checklist

- [ ] Process claims are company-specific or remain unknown.
- [ ] Proposed work has bounded scope and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Early-career support and responsibilities are discussed.
