# Behavioral question bank

## Scope and use

All questions are original **[INFERRED]**, **company-neutral** practice. They are not reports of employer questions. Common target roles: SDE, AI/full-stack engineer, ML engineer and FDE. Context: behavioral or project discussion; candidate level: approximately 0–2 years unless adapted. Difficulty reflects depth of reasoning, not job seniority or company frequency. Advanced incident questions can be answered as explicitly hypothetical scenarios when real experience is absent.

Attempt each prompt aloud before opening its answer guidance. No example below supplies a personal experience to claim. Use [STAR](STAR_Method.md), the [rubric](../00-Interview-Fundamentals/Interview_Evaluation_Rubrics.md), and your actual evidence.

## BHV-01 — Ownership

**Question:** Describe a real problem you followed through beyond the first fix.

- **Category/topic:** Behavioral / Ownership.
- **Difficulty:** Beginner.
- **Target roles:** SDE, AI, full-stack AI, ML and FDE; adapt to actual responsibility.
- **Company/round/level:** Company-neutral; behavioral/project discussion; early career, with scenario depth adjusted.
- **Evaluates:** Initiative within scope, verification and handoff.
- **Related:** BHV-08; search the ID in this document.
- **Source:** Original curriculum prompt; communication guidance in the references.

<details><summary>Expected answer, explanation and followups — open after attempting</summary>

**Expected structure:** Context/task → issue evidence → agreed scope → personal action → checked outcome → handoff.

**Explanation:** Name the actual followup and who owned acceptance.

**Adaptable answer scaffold:** “In [real context], my responsibility was [scope]. I [specific action] because [reason]. [Evidence] showed [actual outcome]. The limit was [uncertainty], and I changed [later behavior].” Fill this with facts; do not recite it as an event.

**Followups:** What remained unresolved? Who had authority?

**Common weak answer:** Confusing long hours with an outcome.

**Strong indicator:** A reviewer can identify your decision, evidence, contribution and reflection without inferring missing facts.

</details>
