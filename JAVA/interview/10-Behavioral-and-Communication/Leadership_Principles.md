# Company values and leadership principles

[OFFICIAL] Amazon publishes Leadership Principles, including Ownership, Customer Obsession, Learn and Be Curious, and Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit. ## Turn values into observable behavior

The table below is [INFERRED] preparation interpretation. It is not an employer's unpublished rubric.

| Theme | Evidence to look for in a real episode | Weak substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Followed a problem through verification and handoff | Worked late without explaining the outcome |
| User/customer focus | Learned the user's need and checked whether the change helped | Assumed any feature was useful |
| Learning | Changed a method after evidence or feedback | Listed courses taken |
| Constructive disagreement | Compared alternatives, listened and supported the decision | Claimed every disagreement was a personal victory |
| Judgment | Balanced speed, risk and reversibility | Treated fast delivery as the only criterion |

## Prepare without forcing stories

Read the target company's actual public values and relevant hiring guidance. Select an episode that shows the behavior naturally. Do not insert a principle's name into every sentence or assume identical values across frontier labs, Indian firms and startups. Those organizations require their own scoped research.

A useful values discussion can include tension: delivering quickly while preserving data correctness; helping another team while meeting your own commitment; disagreeing while respecting decision authority. Explain the tradeoff and the evidence behind your choice.

## Practice

Choose one episode and map it to at most two themes. Explain which observable action supports each interpretation. Then explain what a skeptical reviewer might reasonably question.

<details><summary>Review criteria</summary>

The facts stay fixed when the theme changes. Each theme is tied to an action, and the outcome has evidence. You do not claim a company-specific assessment mechanism or a “correct principle” for a question without support.

</details>
